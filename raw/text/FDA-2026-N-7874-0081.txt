Regulation of generative AI-enabled medical devices must recognize that safe implementation is a shared human responsibility. Governance and oversight should include patients and caregivers; nurses; physicians; pharmacists; other clinicians and allied health professionals; clinical informaticians; biomedical and clinical engineers; data scientists and AI/ML developers; cybersecurity, privacy, and information technology professionals; human-factors and usability experts; quality and patient-safety leaders; ethicists; regulatory and compliance professionals; healthcare administrators and operational leaders; educators; researchers; and device manufacturers. These stakeholders should be involved throughout the AI lifecycle&mdash;from design and validation through implementation, monitoring, reassessment, and retirement&mdash;with clearly defined accountability and meaningful human oversight.
