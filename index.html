How this tracker works

Comments are pulled from the public Regulations.gov docket. AI is used to segment submissions, map passages to the FDA questions they address, classify the position being expressed, and summarize that position in neutral language.

The tracker preserves distinctions among support, support with modification, opposition, mixed positions, and unclear positions rather than reducing comments to simple sentiment.

When a submission makes the same point more than once on the same question, those passages are folded into a single position so one commenter is not counted repeatedly. Passages that take different positions are never merged. Each question shows one card per commenter, with that commenter’s distinct points listed inside it.

Every interpretation remains linked to the source passage and original submission so readers can inspect the evidence directly.

Vahana Labs’ editorial analysis, including commercialization and real-world deployment implications, is visually separated from what commenters themselves said.
